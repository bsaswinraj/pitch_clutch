Tournament rules

Rules of play

Follow these rules throughout the tournament. Play fair, complete your matches on time, and respect your opponent.

01
Match Time
Match duration must be strictly 8 minutes.
02
Substitution
A maximum of 6 substitutions is allowed.
03
Extra Time
Extra time must be set to OFF.
04
Penalty
Penalty must be set to OFF.
05
Condition
Player condition must be set to GOOD.
06
Match Deadline
Matches must be completed strictly before the deadline. Otherwise, the match will be counted as a loss for both teams.
07
Result Submission
The scoreline must be provided along with a screenshot of the result.
08
Fair Play
All matches must be played fair and square.
09
No Spamming
Clear spamming will not be allowed.
Important: All participants are expected to follow the above rules throughout the tournament. Failure to follow the rules may result in penalties or disqualification.

Terms & conditions

By registering for PITCH CLUTCH '26, teams agree to the terms below. This is a starting draft — edit it to match your event's actual policies before going live.

  1. Entry fee

    Registration requires a non-refundable entry fee of ₹40 per team, payable as instructed after registration.

  2. Team composition

    Each team must consist of exactly two members. Both members must provide accurate personal and contact details at the time of registration.

  3. Eligibility

    The tournament is open to all eFootball players. One registration per team; duplicate or fraudulent entries may be disqualified.

  4. Schedule changes

    Match dates, times, and format will be announced separately and may be adjusted by the organizers if required.

  5. Conduct

    Teams are expected to play fairly. Use of exploits, unsporting conduct, or abuse toward opponents or organizers can result in disqualification without refund.

  6. Data use

    Details submitted during registration are used only for organizing and communicating about the tournament, and are not shared with third parties.

  7. Final authority

    The event team's decisions on match results, disputes, and disqualifications are final.
